CalSnap Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

CalSnap ("the app") helps you estimate calories and macros from photos of food. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

1. Information we collect

  • Food photos: when you scan food, the photo is sent to our server and then to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude) so it can estimate the nutrition. We do not store your photos on our servers after the analysis is returned.
  • Your calorie goal and daily log: the calorie goal, scan results and daily log are stored only on your device, in your browser's local storage. We do not receive or keep a copy of them.
  • Subscription status: whether you have premium is stored on your device. Payments, when enabled, are handled by the payment provider and we do not see your card details.

We do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or health records, and we do not use advertising or tracking SDKs.

2. How we use information

We use food photos only to produce the nutrition estimate you asked for. Nothing else is done with them.

3. Third parties

Food photos are processed by Anthropic to generate the estimate. Their handling of data is described in Anthropic's privacy policy. Hosting is provided by Render, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security and operation.

4. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device stays there until you clear your browser or app data, or uninstall the app. Because we keep no account, there is nothing on our side to delete for you. To remove your local data, clear the site data in your browser or uninstall the app.

5. Children

CalSnap is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

6. Health disclaimer

Calorie and macro values are estimates only and may be inaccurate. CalSnap is not medical advice. Consult a doctor or dietitian for health decisions, especially if you have a medical condition.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
